Synced N

Church Music Ministry Schedule & Lineup Management System

SYNCED N

MOBILE APPLICATION

Organizing the music ministry workflow.

Synced N is a proposed mobile application designed to centralize the scheduling and lineup management process of a church music ministry.

Kotlin Android Firebase

01 / OVERVIEW

The idea behind Synced N.

Bringing the music ministry workflow into one centralized system.

Music ministry scheduling can involve coordinating members, assigning teams, preparing song lineups, communicating changes, and making sure everyone has the information needed before a service.

Synced N is being developed to help organize these activities in one place, reducing the need to rely on separate communication and song-reference platforms.

02 / THE PROBLEM

Why build it?

Coordinating people, schedules, and song information can become difficult when information is spread across different platforms.

A music ministry may need to communicate team assignments, member availability, song selections, keys, chords, lineup changes, and rehearsal information.

Synced N aims to provide a centralized space where members can access the information relevant to their upcoming worship service.

03 / PLANNED FEATURES
