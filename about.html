02 / BIOGRAPHY & PROFILE

A little about me.

Bridging engineering rigor with digital creative discipline.

I am Joshua Aquino Lagare, known publicly as JUSWA — a Computer Engineering student, software developer, and digital artist.

My journey began with a natural curiosity for how systems operate beneath the surface: from raw electrical signals and microcontrollers executing machine code to high-level mobile applications connecting communities across cloud databases.

Rather than viewing engineering and artistic creativity as separate disciplines, I treat them as complementary lenses. Engineering gives structure, logic, and functional reliability; art gives visual intentionality, empathy, and aesthetic character.

02.1 / FOCUS AREAS

What drives my work.

Software & Mobile

01

I develop mobile and web solutions with an emphasis on maintainable architecture and responsive design. My work on Synced N in Kotlin with Android Studio and Firebase demonstrates my focus on solving practical coordination and scheduling problems.

Embedded & IoT

02

Physical computing is central to my Computer Engineering studies. Working with Arduino Nano and ESP32 systems, I integrate ultrasonic distance sensors, RTC time modules, and OLED displays to build assistive technologies like the Smart Cane and Smart Medication System.

Digital Art

03

Outside code and hardware circuits, I paint digital character illustrations in Krita. Studying anatomy, perspective, neon rim-lighting, and dynamic composition feeds directly back into my understanding of visual hierarchy and user interface design.
